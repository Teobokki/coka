# COKA — CALL OF KIMS ARMY

An army-camp training shooter from EX Inc./HYPERION DIGITAL. You play **Ana**, a recruit of Kims Army at Camp Kim.

- **First-person** gunplay: arms and weapon viewmodel, iron sights / red dot / 4x scope ADS, weapon bob and sway, recoil kick, hit markers, grenades and knife.
- **Campaign**: 6 voiced missions (Basic Training, Kill House, Night Ops, Defend the Camp, Convoy Escort, Final Assault) with checkpoints and cinematic intros.
- **COKA ONLINE AI TRAINING**: Team Deathmatch 6v6 and Free-for-all 8 against named AI bots (Recruit / Regular / Veteran), scoreboard and match XP. Runs offline.
- **Shooting range** with pop-up targets.
- **RPG progression**: 11 ranks (Recruit to Commander), unlockable fictional weapons, attachments, digital camos and perks.
- **Graphics**: RTX and EX HD modes, golden-hour camp with real sun shadows, dust haze with sun in-scattering, filmic grade and grain (no bloom).

Controls: WASD move (A left, D right), mouse aim, LMB fire, RMB aim down sights, Shift sprint, C / Ctrl crouch (slide while sprinting), Space jump,
R reload, G grenade, V knife, F use, 1/2 or wheel switch weapon, L helmet light (night), Tab scoreboard, Esc pause.

Windows: per-user installer (no admin) or portable zip. Saves in `%APPDATA%\EX Inc\COKA`.
